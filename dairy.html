DAIRY

We are equipped to handle the needs for all dairies in Northern Wisconsin. We utilize the newest technology with our portable ultrasound machines to detect pregnancies early, fast and accurately, as well as definitively diagnose reproductive diseases. We are capable of detecting twins, viable fetuses and fetal sexing. Aside from pregnancy diagnostics, we can help accelerate your breeding program by offering consulting and protocol development.
