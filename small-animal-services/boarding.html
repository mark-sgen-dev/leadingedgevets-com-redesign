Check-ins & Check-outs

Canine Requirements

**Needs to be completed 2 weeks prior to their stay.

  • DAPP (distemper vaccine)
  • Bordetella
  • Rabies
  • Heartworm Test (negative)
  • Fecal (negative)
  • *Current on flea/tick & heartworm prevention.

Feline Requirements

  • Rabies
  • FVRCP
  • Fecal (negative)
  • *Current on flea/tick & heartworm prevention.

RATES

  • Pricing is applied each calendar day​
  • ​5.5% Wisconsin State Tax will be added to all pricing at check out,
  • along with a 3% convenience fee for credit cards.
  • Dog$32
  • Additional Dog$26.50
  • Cat$25.00
  • Additional Cat$20.00
  • Medication Administration$3.00
  • In House Diet$2.75 per meal
  • Day Board M-F$28.00

Personal belongings are allowed but condition upon return is not guaranteed.

We ask that you provide your pets normal diet for the duration of their stay and a little extra just in case. This reduces the risk of GI upset during and after boarding stays
