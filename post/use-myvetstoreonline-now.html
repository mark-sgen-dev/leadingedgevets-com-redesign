Use MyVetStoreOnline Now!

Dr. Mickelson, DVMDec 10, 2018 1 min read

We have just added a convenient online veterinary supply store! Through Midwest Veterinary Supply you can now order medication for your pets online.

Any over the counter products can be ordered straight from the website!

Utilize 'Easy Dose It!" to ensure accurate, timely medication administration to your four legged friend. Each month, a single dose of preventative medication is mailed directly to you! And shipping is free!

Scheduled orders: in addition to monthly preventatives, you can easily set recurring orders of any product available on myvetstoreonline. From bottles of medication to bags of food, scheduled ordering allows you to plan in advance.

Go to myvetstoreonline.com today and register!!!
