Equine Reproduction Services

Our veterinary clinic offers a range of reproduction services including breeding management, breeding soundness exams, fresh/frozen semen analysis, and artificial insemination for your horse.

Breeding Management & Exams

Breeding Soundness Exams are essential for proper care and maintenance during pregnancy and ensures mare and foal health.

Semen Analysis

We offer fresh or frozen semen for shipping or artificial insemination at the clinic. We also offer semen analysis for effective shipping and breeding with fresh or frozen semen.

Artifical Insemination

We offer ultrasound guided, artificial insemination at the clinic with fresh or cooled semen via synchronized breeding.
