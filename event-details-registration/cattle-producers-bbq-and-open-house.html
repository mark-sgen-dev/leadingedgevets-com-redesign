About the event

Back in 2020, Leading Edge was the recipient of a Veterinary Services Grant through the USDA. The Veterinary Services Grant Program was designed to develop, implement, and sustain food animal veterinary services through education, training, recruitment, placement, and retention of veterinarians in rural areas. Veterinarians are critical to America’s food safety and security, as well as to the health and well-being of both animals and humans. Leading Edge has been providing large animal veterinary services for all bovine producers (Dairy & Beef) for over 25 years. This grant is intended to support that practice through the purchase of vehicles, equipment and fund events. If you have cattle of any kind (Dairy or Beef) we encourage you to attend this event. You do not need to be a current bovine client to attend. We just ask for an RSVP in order to be best accommodating.
