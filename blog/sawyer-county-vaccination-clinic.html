Sawyer County Vaccination Clinic

Amber SpiegelMar 6 1 min read
  • All News
  • Small Animal
  • Dairy
  • Equine
  • Livestock
  • Events

We are again working in conjunction with Sawyer County to host a low-cost rabies vaccination & microchipping clinic. *Cash & Check Payments Only* Rabies $15 Microchip $20

*Annual County Dog Licenses are also required at these events.*

Spayed & Neutered Fee:$10

Unaltered/Intact Fee: $15

Friday, March 20th 1-4 PM @ Winter Fire Hall

5130 Crawford Street, Winter, WI 54896

Saturday, March 21st 9 AM-1 PM @ City of Hayward Fire Hall

10555 Dakota Avenue, Hayward, WI 54843

Pre-registering for the event will save you time the day of. Call 715-934-9055 to pre-register.

Remember to bring a prior vaccination certificate to be issued a 3 year vaccine. Any pet without prior proof of vaccination will only be issued a 1 year vaccine.
