Beef, Swine and Small Ruminants

Leading Edge Veterinary Services

We provide services for beef cattle, goats, sheep, alpacas, pigs and poultry. Routine vaccines, deworming protocols, castrations and all your emergency needs are provided through our clinic.

**With our available haul-in facilities at our clinic, please call us to schedule an appointment**

We have stocks, and a chute system and head gate for producer, veterinarian and animal safety.
