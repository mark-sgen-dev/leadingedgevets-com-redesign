LARGE ANIMAL SERVICES

Leading Edge Veterinary Services is proud to provide all-encompassing, high quality veterinary medical needs to all animals in northern Wisconsin. Our large animal team consists of Dr. Ed Metcalf and Dr. Roedel. Their work is primarily out of their vehicles, traveling to the equine and farming communities of Northern Wisconsin. We do offer haul-in appointments at the clinic during normal business hours, where we have a chute system, stocks and more available equipment to provide more thorough care.

DAIRY

From herd healths and feed directives to exams and breeding, we cover all sizes of dairy operations.

EQUINE

We have all your equine needs covered. Coggins, dental, ultrasounds and vaccinations are just a few services we provide.

Beef, Swine and Small Ruminants

Find all your preventative and emergency medical livestock needs here. We provide services for beef cattle, goats, sheep, alpacas, pigs and poultry. Routine vaccines, deworming protocols, castrations and all your emergency needs are provided for at one stop.
